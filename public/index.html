About His-Story

Discover what His-Story is, why it exists, and what readers can find on the platform.

What is His-Story?

His-Story is a digital platform created to bring history and storytelling closer to readers. We believe that the past is more than a collection of dates and names. It is made up of people, places, cultures, kingdoms, discoveries, struggles, achievements and events that continue to shape the world.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make history and storytelling accessible through carefully prepared digital books. His-Story aims to preserve important histories while also creating original stories that entertain, inspire and communicate meaningful lessons.

What Readers Can Find

Readers can explore histories from Ghana, Africa and around the world, learn about important historical figures and events, discover civilizations and cultures, and enjoy original stories filled with adventure, mystery, wisdom and imagination.

Our goal is simple: to help history remain remembered and stories remain alive.
