About His-Story

A place where the past is remembered and stories continue to live.

✦ Why His-Story?

His-Story is a digital library created to make history and original storytelling easier to discover and enjoy.

The History section explores people, civilizations, kingdoms, cultures, places, discoveries and important events from around the world.

The Stories section brings readers original stories that combine imagination, mystery, wisdom and meaningful lessons.

History helps us understand where we came from. Stories help us imagine where we can go.
